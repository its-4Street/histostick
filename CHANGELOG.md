# Changelog

## 0.1.0, 2026-11-19

First public version.

- The chart: band bars with counts, a smallest-to-largest wick and a median tick per column.
- Bands of any kind (`bands`), on a log or linear scale, with bar width by count or by share of the column.
- The x-axis can be reordered, grouped under brackets, or combined group by group.
- A two-handle year slider when the values carry dates.
- The band axis stays pinned when the chart scrolls sideways.
- Four demonstration charts: SEC respondents, fictional home prices, NVDA daily moves, blood pressure of US adults.
- No packages, no build step.
